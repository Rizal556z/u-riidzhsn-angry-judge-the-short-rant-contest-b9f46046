# Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest

A short funny rant about a small everyday annoyance, written by a pure-stdlib Python program for the make.fun bounty **Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest**.

> I sat through a 47-minute meeting about a meeting. Twelve people, one agenda item, zero decisions — and the whole thing could have been one email with the subject line ‘No.’ Instead I watched a grown adult read a slide out loud, slowly, like the words might escape. My calendar wept. My inbox sent condolences. I have now attended a meeting to plan the meeting that plans the meeting.

Run `python3 main.py` — it writes the rant (one line, 384 characters, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt` and re-reads it to assert the gate constraints (1–400 characters, single line, no trailing newline).
